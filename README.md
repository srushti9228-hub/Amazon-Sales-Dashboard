## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard** is a Tableau-based data visualization project built to explore sales performance across regions, countries, channels, order priorities, item types, and shipping costs.

The workbook contains **3 dashboard pages**:
1. **Sales Homepage** — high-level KPIs and geographic profitability.
2. **Executive Page** — channel analysis, order priority, yearly orders, and Pareto revenue analysis.
3. **Product Insights** — top item types by profit and orders, plus yearly shipping-cost trends.

The goal is to turn sales data into clear, decision-ready visual insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Track key sales KPIs in one place.
- Compare profit across regions and countries.
- Analyze online and offline sales-channel contribution.
- Review yearly order patterns and shipping-cost trends.
- Identify leading item types by profit and order count.
- Explore cumulative revenue contribution with Pareto analysis.
- Present business performance through a clean, multi-page dashboard.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the values visible in the supplied dashboard screenshots:

- **Offline revenue is higher** at approximately **$1,425.39M**, compared with **$984.28M** from Online.
- **Cosmetics leads the displayed Top 5 profit chart**, at approximately **$288.1M**.
- **Cosmetics also leads the displayed Top 5 orders chart**, with **261 orders**.
- **Sub-Saharan Africa** is the highest region in the visible profit ranking, at approximately **$225.4M**.
- The displayed shipping-cost trend reaches its highest point in **2012**, at approximately **$389.6M**.
- The Pareto view helps examine how cumulative revenue contribution builds across item types.

*These are observations from the dashboard visuals; they do not establish the causes behind the results.*
